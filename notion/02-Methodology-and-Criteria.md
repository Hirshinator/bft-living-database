# Methodology & Criteria

How the database decides **who's in**, **how they're rated**, and the standards that keep it credible. This page is the plain-language summary; the live database holds the full rule set.

## How people are classified

Every person gets one **stance**:

- **pro** — an ally, whether by explicit statement or clear team-alignment.
- **hostile** — actively anti-Israel or antisemitic.
- **neutral** — no clear lean, or genuinely mixed.
- **yellowflag / redflag** — an ally (or potential ally) carrying a caution worth surfacing (e.g. a contested record or association). Not the same as hostile.

Each person also gets an **Alliance Score (1–100)** — intensity *within* their stance, so we can rank allies rather than treat them as equal. A rock-solid ally might be 95; a coalition-of-convenience ally, 55.

## Who counts as an ally

Evidence doesn't have to be a pro-Israel tweet. These all count as real positive signal:

- **Team-alignment** — coalition allies against the anti-Israel left generally count as allies, even without an explicit Israel statement.
- **Silent-majority rule** — business leaders usually won't post about Israel publicly; that silence is *normal*, not a negative. (For media figures who talk politics constantly, silence is more meaningful.)
- **Philosemitic / institutional proximity** — amplifying Jewish talent, Yeshiva University / Tikvah / Hertog ties, or sharing institutional homes with confirmed allies.
- **Relational inference** — a Jewish spouse, family, or close mentors are a real signal (used carefully, never as sole proof).

## The two gates (for influencers)

An influencer is strongest when they clear **both**:

- **Gate 1 — Loyalty:** what did their advocacy *cost* them? Voluntary support that drew backlash (especially pre-Oct-7) is worth far more than a safe, paid, or late statement.
- **Gate 2 — Talent:** can they actually hold an audience (long-form, interviewed, not just reading a script)?

Related weighting rules:

- **Organic beats paid** — the earliest-aligned, sub-threshold voices outrank late or sponsored ones.
- **Anonymous channels are demoted** (exception: large *and* nameable, like Libs of TikTok).

## Ranking rules

- **Nationality:** American > close-ally nation > other > Israeli — because the world reads non-American advocacy as biased. *Where the audience is* can outweigh nationality.
- **Charisma, intelligence, and actual influence can override** the above — real reach and persuasiveness are the point.

## Political & government (evidence, not opinion)

Officials are scored on their **documented record**, not vibes:

- **Red line:** voting against defense funding for Israel crosses a red line.
- **Genocide / apartheid test:** calling Israel an "apartheid state," or the Gaza war "genocide," without retraction → hostile.
- **How it's built:** legislators are added from their actual votes and bill cosponsorships (official Congress data + Senate roll-call records) — every rating traces to specific bills and votes.

## Hostiles & the discovery flywheel

We track hostiles too — because they *power discovery*:

- **Enemy vs. rival** — a true enemy is different from a mere disagreement.
- **Enemy-of-my-enemy** is a lead, handled with caution, not an automatic ally.
- **The flywheel:** once enough advocates *and* hostiles are mapped, monitoring their channels surfaces new advocates (who they platform) and new hostiles (who they attack) → add those → monitor them → repeat.

## Nurture pipeline (finding tomorrow's stars)

- **Priority band:** creators with **4,000–20,000 total followers** across all platforms — proven traction, but early enough to cultivate before they're expensive or spoken-for.
- **Seed early, back the breakouts** — track growth from a baseline; graduate breakouts into full influencers.

## Standards that keep it credible

- **Primary sources first**, most recent preferred, with a citable link wherever possible.
- **"Needs human verification"** flag on anything added without conclusive evidence, so reviewers can find every soft entry at a glance.
- **Every change is validated before it goes live.**

## Data ethics

- **Public figures only.** We never compile dossiers on private individuals.
- **Keep stance ratings and contact details separate**, in separately-permissioned places (see the Security page).
