# BFT Evangelicals Program — Project Overview

## What this is
A living intelligence database mapping the pro-Israel / evangelical-Zionist alliance ecosystem for **Builders for Tomorrow**. It is both an operational tool and a **pitch artifact** for donors who funded evangelical outreach before without seeing returns.

## Johnnie Moore's three-legged stool
1. **Modernise communications infrastructure.** Evangelicals *under-index in podcasting*; Catholics and libertarians over-index. Concrete target: find people who already have audiences but are **not** in podcasting and move them there.
2. **Integrate AND coordinate** the Jewish and Christian communities — joint philanthropy, a cross-community advisory board, reciprocal recruiting.
3. **Education.** Pepperdine, Liberty's Center for Israel; bolder ideas: American Christians doing undergrad in Israel, Technion scholarships.

## Strategic principles (from the interviews)
- The evangelical community is **grassroots and bottom-up, not hierarchical** — "like a free market." No leader can endorse and cascade it down. High-trust, so momentum is hard to start and very high leverage once achieved.
- **Jews should not be publicly front-facing** (Dana, explicit). Elevate authentic Christian voices; Jewish involvement stays behind the scenes.
- **Asymmetric strategy, humour, trolling.** Johnnie: disinformation "is not about the facts, it's about community." He praises the Babylon Bee — roughly 30% more effective simply by being funny and smart. Fact-checking alone loses.

## The strategic gap still unbuilt
Johnnie described wanting **global, city-based strategic databases**: pick ~20 strategic cities, map the large churches, and identify the strategic **lay members** — top attorneys, doctors, school-board members — indexed by sector for rapid activation. "Most of which has never been done before."
Our database is US-centric, media-influencer-centric and clergy-centric. His vision is global, church-based, lay-professional and sector-indexed. **These are complementary, not the same thing.**
