## 1. Criteria & rules (the system logic)

**Inclusion / exclusion**
- **Government red line:** any public official who *voted against* defense funding for Israel crosses a red line (out); anyone who voted *for* it counts in favor. Applies to past & present congressional, gubernatorial, state, local, and federal records, for all living officials.
- **Genocide/apartheid test:** anyone who called Israel an "apartheid state," or called the war in Gaza "genocide," and has **not** retracted → hostile.
- **Enemy vs. rival:** distinguish a true enemy from a mere rival. "Enemy of my enemy" and "rival of my rival" can both surface allies.
- **Signals of allyship to auto-flag:** wearing the **yellow hostage ribbon** or the **blue square** anti-antisemitism pin; using "Judeo-Christian" positively/neutrally; being featured by the **Genesis Prize**; positive posts about a small set of pro-Israel figures; supporting Reza Pahlavi (indicator of Jewish/Israel allyship).
- **Attacks-as-signal:** boost anyone who *voluntarily* pushed back on Candace Owens, Tucker Carlson, Nick Fuentes, or Sneako.
- **Removed criterion:** stop flagging anti-Muslim rhetoric on its own.

**Ranking / weighting**
- **Organic > paid:** volunteer advocacy (on their own or a non-Jewish platform) outranks what looks like a paid speaking engagement.
- **Americans > international:** the world reads non-American advocacy as biased, so US natives who back Israel are the priority. International figures can be ranked by how close an ally their country is to the US — but this is a soft rule.
- **Charisma/intelligence/actual-influence override:** these can outweigh the nationality rule (e.g. Ollie Anisfeld — British & Jewish but sharp, media-savvy, theologically fluent → ranks higher).
- **Emerging > established, sometimes:** rising/early influencers under ~50K followers go in a dedicated "Nurture / emerging" bucket; the earliest-aligned content and organic growth are worth more than late, paid alignment.
- **Anonymous channels demoted** (exception: big *and* nameable, like Libs of TikTok).
- **Evidence required:** attach a link for the ideological read where possible (post sentiment, voting record, an interview clip).

**Structural distinctions**
- Separate **influencer**, **business leader / B2B**, and **grassroots** networks.
- Sub-categorize influencers: **sports, music, film, media, comedy, entertainment**.
- When an ally is confirmed, also add their **orgs/companies, past companies, friendly interviewers/interviewees, family, and all social links**.
- **Map ally media companies as networks:** for a *confirmed* ally brand, add its sub-channels and recurring collaborators — but a shared parent or a collaboration is a *lead*, never proof (the collaboration graph cuts both ways).

## 2. Discovery methods & sources to mine

- **Roster sources:** IAF "Israel's Top-50 Christian Allies" (2021–2025), Genesis Prize honorees, Free Press masthead, Federalist Society / Hamilton Society / Washington Institute / JINSA / Hoover / CNAS networks, freedom-conservatism signatories, AIPAC / DMFI / RJC–supported figures, Commentary (Podhoretz) circle, Abraham & Isaac Accords participants.
- **Relational inference:** who a figure hosts, guests with, comments on positively, married into, or mentors reveals ideology. Scan ally social pages for the non-Jewish, philosemitic voices they platform.
- **Comment-network / bellwether:** positive sentiment on an ally's post → grassroots/emerging pipeline; negative sentiment on a *hostile's* post → enemy-of-enemy allies. (Feasible with a logged-in session; not at scale logged-out.)
- **Reverse Canary Mission** (an anti-Israel doxxing list) = an enemy-compiled ally roster. Mine public figures only; never mirror doxxing.
- **B2B net:** capital firms (VC/PE/growth/RE) with Israel offices, invested in Israeli companies, that acquired an Israeli company, or with a Jewish/Zionist/Evangelical/devout-Protestant principal.

## 4. Strategy, coalition & program ideas

- **Core thesis:** map the entire pro-Israel / philosemitic media, entertainment, political, and business landscape (plus enemies) and their interconnections — then monitor sentiment in real time to catch emerging influencers early.
- **Target coalition:** MAGA + moderate/corporate Democrats (the ~20% of Democrats still openly pro-Israel — Fetterman, Torres, Galloway, Tusk, Maher archetypes), explicitly *isolating* the Tucker/Fuentes/Webbon nativist-right wing and the DSA left. "From the Free Press to the Daily Wire."
- **Coalition branding (brainstorm):** "Burgundy Coalition" (#660033), plus alternates considered (Slivovitz/Plum-Brandy, "silent majority / common sense"). A "Where's Waldo" umbrella cartoon with figures sized by importance to the coalition.
- **Rising-star focus:** identifying and amplifying emerging Gen-Z/Alpha pro-Israel leaders is the *front-line* goal (early examples flagged: Sam Brown; the man who confronted Patriot Front on the DC Metro; David Wood's son) — invite to fellowship/events.
- **Wedge ideas:** Native American outreach; former-Muslim & convert networks; Noahide network; the joint anti-pornography / cyber-safety angle (Christian + Jewish filter orgs + Israeli cyber like Allot/Radiant, paired with lobbying).
- **B2B / program:** VC-in-Residence + fellowship; Tamid-style chapters in Christian universities (Liberty, Baylor, Pepperdine); AI partnerships (YU–Baylor–Notre Dame–BYU); a "Pro-Israel Tech Stack" of partner tools offering perks to members (guard against leaks).
